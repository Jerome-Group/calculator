# Product scope

Calculator provides durable, device-local symbolic and numerical computation across undergraduate mathematics. A narrow desktop pane and portrait phone are ordinary working spaces.

One notebook connects editable mathematical input, reusable objects, history and graphs. Computation must remain cancellable, distinguish exact/numeric/conditional/unresolved/invalid outcomes, and retain domain restrictions and explicit bounds. No runtime AI or calculation service is required.

ChatGPT sign-in and account-scoped cloud sync support continuity across devices. Local computation and previously saved work remain available after offline preparation; exported backups provide durable user-controlled copies. Sign-out must invalidate in-flight account work and cached authenticated shells.

Coverage is bounded by mature bundled engines, finite representations and explicit numerical domains. This is a calculation tool, with no claim of universal theorem proving or solutions to arbitrary symbolic problems. See COVERAGE_RESEARCH.md and the in-app coverage document.
